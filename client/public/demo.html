🛡️ Client Confidence & Trust Matrix
Direct answers to the top 4 concerns restaurant operators ask before upgrading.
📶 What if our internet drops during Saturday dinner rush?
Zero downtime. QuantroBill operates on an offline-resilient architecture. Bills, running orders, and KOTs continue punching seamlessly in local browser storage, auto-syncing with zero data loss the second connection recovers.
🔒 Can cashiers manipulate prices or pocket cash?
Strictly prevented. Bill cancellations, item voids, and discounts require an Admin Supervisor PIN override. Every action is recorded in the tamper-evident day-end audit register with exact timestamps and staff IDs.
👨‍🍳 Can non-tech kitchen staff learn to use this?
5-minute onboarding. Designed specifically for high-stress Indian kitchens. Big color-coded touch buttons, intuitive food dish icons, and distinct audio chimes mean helpers and stewards master it on their first shift.
🖨️ Do we need to buy expensive proprietary hardware?
Works with your existing hardware. Runs on any standard Windows PC, touchscreen POS, Android tablet, iPad, USB/LAN/Bluetooth thermal receipt printer (ESC/POS 80mm/58mm), and USB barcode scanner.
